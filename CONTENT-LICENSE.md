# Original sample content

This dedication does not apply to commercial manga, publisher marks, linked website content, or imported user files. No third-party font or cover image is bundled. System fonts are selected by the reader's device.

Summaries are original concise descriptions based on cited official work pages. See research.json for source verification and the separate scope of reading and redistribution permissions.
